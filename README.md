## 💡 The Idea

Small neighborhood businesses — bakeries, markets, barbershops — often struggle to have a digital presence. They rely on word of mouth or informal WhatsApp groups, but have no centralized place for customers to find them.

**Vizinho Digital** is a concept platform that gives these businesses a simple, accessible way to register, create a storefront, and connect with local customers.

> *Inspired by real businesses in Canoinhas, SC — where the gap between small commerce and digital tools is very real.*

---

## ✨ Features

- 🏪 **Business registration** — merchants sign up and create their digital profile
- 🛍️ **Store page** — each business gets its own public-facing storefront
- 📊 **Merchant dashboard** — panel for managing store information
- ✍️ **Description generator** — helps merchants write their business description
- 📱 **WhatsApp integration concept** — designed with local mobile-first users in mind
